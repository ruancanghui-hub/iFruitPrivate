Fruit Storm Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026
Publisher: Canghui Ruan (阮沧晖)
Privacy and support contact: ruancanghui@163.com

This policy describes how Canghui Ruan handles information in Fruit Storm (水果风暴), the iOS game identified by com.nightelf.iFruit, and when you contact support or visit our policy and support pages.

Playing the game

Fruit Storm is a single-player puzzle game. You do not need an account to play. Your progress is stored in the app's storage on your device, including unlocked levels, earned stars, scores, in-game juice, lives and their recovery time, booster inventory, and music and sound settings. The app uses this information to continue your game and apply your preferences.

The game reads your preferred device language to display English or Simplified Chinese. Unsupported language preferences fall back to English. This choice is processed on your device.

The game has no account login or developer-operated cloud save service. Device backups you enable may include app data and can restore it later. Those backups are controlled through your Apple device and account settings. Apple's explanation of iCloud Backup

Ads, payments, and sensitive permissions

The game does not integrate an advertising service or real-money in-app purchases. Supplies are exchanged for in-game juice. The game does not request your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, or Bluetooth access for gameplay.

Analytics, diagnostics, and tracking

Fruit Storm runs on the Unity game engine. We have disabled Unity's optional analytics, advertising, and cloud crash/performance-reporting services for this game. We do not use game activity to build advertising profiles or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Apple handles App Store distribution, device backups, and device diagnostics you choose to share under its own services and your Apple settings. Apple Privacy Policy

When you contact us

If you email ruancanghui@163.com, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and attachments. We use it to answer your request and resolve related support issues. The email services involved in delivering and storing the correspondence also process that message. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other information that is unnecessary for your request.

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and related issues, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete it using the same contact address. We do not sell support correspondence or use it for personalized advertising.

Policy and support website

Our policy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security when those pages are visited, including visits without a GitHub login. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of website information.

Retention and your choices

The game keeps local progress and preferences so you can resume playing. You can change music and sound settings in the game. You can remove the app's local data by using your device's Delete App action; simply offloading the app may preserve its data. Copies in device backups are managed separately through the backup service and may be restored.

We cannot remotely erase copies held only on your device or in a backup service you control. For information you have sent to support, contact ruancanghui@163.com to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to any retention requirements that apply to that information.

Children

The game does not ask players to enter personal details. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact ruancanghui@163.com so we can review and address the request. The App Store age rating is stated separately on the product page.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our information practices change and show the new effective date on the published page.

Questions about this policy: Canghui Ruan (阮沧晖), ruancanghui@163.com.
