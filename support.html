Fruit Storm Support

For questions, feedback, or help with Fruit Storm, contact 阮沧晖 at ruancanghui@163.com.

Reporting a problem

Tell us your device model, iOS version, game version, and the steps that led to the problem. You may attach a screenshot if it helps explain the issue. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and other unnecessary personal information.

Progress and settings

Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Fruit Storm does not provide a game account or its own cloud save service. Deleting the app can remove local progress; backups are managed through your device and Apple account settings.

The game uses Simplified Chinese when your device’s first preferred language is Chinese, and English for other preferred languages. Music and sound effects can be adjusted separately in the game’s Settings.

See the Privacy Policy for information about local saves, support messages, and this website.

进度与设置

游戏进度和偏好保存在本机。水果风暴不提供游戏账号或自有云存档服务。删除 App 可能移除本地进度；设备备份由你的设备和 Apple 账号设置管理。

设备的首选语言为中文时，游戏显示简体中文；其他首选语言显示英文。音乐与音效可在游戏设置中分别调整。
